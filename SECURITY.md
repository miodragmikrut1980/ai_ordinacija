# Security and clinical-safety reporting

Do not put patient names, documents, screenshots, passwords, encryption keys,
or TOTP codes in a public issue. Send security or privacy incidents only to
the clinic's designated security owner using its approved protected channel.

For a suspected account compromise: disable the account, revoke its sessions,
reset its MFA factor through a different administrator, preserve audit logs,
and follow the clinic's incident-response and legal notification process.

For a suspected clinical-safety defect: stop relying on the affected feature,
record the exact version and non-identifying reproduction evidence, and have a
qualified clinician review any patient-impacting decision independently. This
tool is decision support; it never replaces professional clinical judgement.
